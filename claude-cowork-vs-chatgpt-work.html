The real question

Do you need to own it?

Claude Cowork and ChatGPT Work solve the same problem the same way. The fork in the road is ownership.

Both closed platforms keep your configuration in their product and your data in their cloud. The open-source third way — Kortix (Suna) — puts agents, skills, memory, and connectors in a git repo you own, and runs them on hardware you control. If sovereignty matters, the choice isn't between Claude and ChatGPT; it's between closed and open.
